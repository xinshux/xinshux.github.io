About关于我

I work on the probabilities of causation — deriving what counterfactual quantities a dataset can and cannot identify, and how tight those bounds get. 我研究因果概率——一份数据究竟能识别出哪些反事实量、识别不出哪些，以及这些界能收紧到什么程度。

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Florida State University, in the Causal Intelligence Lab, working on causal inference since 2024. My thesis work derives closed-form expressions for PNS, PS and PN in multivariate settings and proves those forms complete, and establishes ε-identifiability conditions for causal effects, ETT, PN and PS under weak structural assumptions. 我在佛罗里达州立大学因果智能实验室（CIL）攻读计算机科学博士，自 2024 年起从事因果推断研究。论文工作的两条主线是：在多变量场景下推导 PNS、PS、PN 的闭式表达式并证明其完备性；以及在弱结构假设下建立因果效应、ETT、PN 与 PS 的 ε-可识别性条件。

The work has appeared at IJCAI and AISTATS, the latter co-authored with Turing Award laureate Judea Pearl. Before the Ph.D. I spent two years doing quantitative and behavioral modeling at Columbia and in industry, which is where the interest in high-noise, real-world business settings comes from: identification theory is only useful if it survives contact with data that was never designed for it. 相关成果发表于 IJCAI 与 AISTATS，后者与图灵奖得主 Judea Pearl 合作完成。读博之前，我在哥伦比亚大学和业界做了两年定量与行为建模，对高噪声的真实业务场景的兴趣正来自那段经历：识别理论只有在面对并非为它而生的数据时仍然站得住，才真正有用。

I am glad to hear from people working on identifiability, counterfactual reasoning, or applying causal methods to messy observational data. Email is the fastest way to reach me. 欢迎做可识别性、反事实推理，或把因果方法用在杂乱观测数据上的同行来信交流，邮件是最快的联系方式。

Theory理论

Closed-Form Probabilities of Causation因果概率的闭式刻画

Derived closed-form expressions for PNS, PS and PN in multivariate settings, replacing the recursive bounds previously used, and proved the derived forms complete. 在多变量场景下推导出 PNS、PS、PN 的闭式表达式，取代此前使用的递归界，并证明了这些表达式的完备性。

Theory理论

ε-Identifiability Conditionsε-可识别性条件

Established ε-identifiability conditions for causal effects, ETT, PN and PS under weak structural assumptions, widening the set of questions answerable from observational data alone. 在弱结构假设下建立了因果效应、ETT、PN 与 PS 的 ε-可识别性条件，扩大了仅凭观测数据即可回答的问题范围。

Built structural causal models characterizing causal relationships between brain regions from neural electrophysiological recordings. 基于神经电生理信号构建结构因果模型，刻画脑区之间的因果关系。
